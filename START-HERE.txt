FORMA 0.8

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE (recommended)
1. Open Forma's web address on your phone in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
2. In Forma go to Settings > App & backups > Install, or use the browser
   menu > Install app / Add to home screen. (iPhone: Safari > Share >
   Add to Home Screen.)
3. Forma now opens full screen from its icon and works offline.
   Long-press the icon for shortcuts: Log activity, Quick start, Progress.
When a new version is published, Forma shows "A new version is ready".
Tap Update now; your data stays where it is.

OR OPEN IT AS A FILE
Open Forma.html in any modern browser. No installation or account needed.

BRINGING YOUR DATA ACROSS
- Same browser, same computer as the previous Forma.html: your routines,
  plans, history and any unfinished session are picked up automatically.
  The old copy is left untouched, so you can always go back.
- Anywhere else (or from the online version): in the old version choose
  Export backup, then in this version go to Settings > Restore backup and
  pick that file. Backups from the previous version restore fine.

SAVING AND PRIVACY
Everything autosaves in this browser on this device, and nowhere else:
there is no server and no account. Progress photos too. The installed app asks the browser to
protect its data from automatic clean-up. Back up now and then from
Settings > App & backups ("Share backup" sends it to Google Drive, email
and so on). A backup is also how you move your data to another device.
Clearing browser data or switching browsers can make local saves
unavailable. A reload recovers an active session, paused where you left it.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.3
- Real tracking: log actual reps, weight and distance per set, with
  "last time" hints and weight carried to the remaining sets.
- Log activities done outside the app (runs, classes, sports) and mark
  planned sessions as done. Your week shows what is planned and what is done.
- Progress: minutes per week, streak, personal bests, per-exercise history,
  full history with notes and effort, CSV export.
- 228 exercises (118 new): barbell, dumbbell, kettlebell, cable/machines,
  calisthenics, HIIT, running/rowing/swimming with distance, mobility, yoga
  and guided breathing. Filter by the equipment you own.
- 13 templates, incl. push/pull/legs, full-body barbell, Tabata, run-walk
  intervals, morning mobility and breathing.
- Quick start: interval timer, meditation with breathing guides, "do it again".
- Routine editor: searchable exercise picker, duplicate steps, named repeat
  groups (circuits, supersets), notes per exercise, unsaved-changes guard.
- Player: back button, countdown beeps, keeps the screen awake, keyboard
  shortcuts (Space = pause, Enter = done), finish summary with effort + notes.
- Settings: kg/lb, week start, light/dark theme, sounds, default rest.

OFFLINE
Everything works offline. Forma makes no connections to other websites
(it uses the device's own font). Only image/video links you add to custom
exercises load from the internet.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.4
- Installable app with its own icon, offline mode, update notice,
  home-screen shortcuts, storage protection and backup sharing.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.5
- Feels more like an app: tapping text no longer highlights it.
- Routine editor: drag steps by their grip handle to reorder them, also
  into and out of repeat groups (arrow keys work on a focused grip).
- Exercise picker: the library's search, activity chips, filters and
  sorting (suggested, A to Z, most used, recently done, by muscle) with
  section headings, body maps, your last result, details without leaving
  the picker, a ✓ count per exercise, and Undo for the last add.
- The library gained the same sorting and headings.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.6
- Colours: Settings > Colours picks Forest, Rose, Lavender, Ocean or
  Kitty (soft pinks with kitten stickers). Each works in light and dark.
- Progressive overload: the weight and reps you actually did are saved
  back into the routine when you finish. The summary shows what changed,
  with "Keep my previous plan instead" to undo.
- Note for next time: write it in the finish summary; it pops up (and
  can be edited) before you start that routine again. No note, no popup.
- The phone's back button now steps back (closes the picker, a dialog,
  asks before leaving a session, returns to My week) instead of leaving.
- New templates: Core A (front and deep core) and Core B (sides and
  rotation), about 15 minutes each. Alternate them twice a week.
- The player no longer shows the help line under the controls.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.7
- Body: a new tab for weight and body measurements on a figure. Each
  spot shows your latest number and how it changed (since the start,
  3 months, 1 month or last time). Tap a spot for its chart and history.
- Check-ins walk you through weight, each spot, optional photos and a
  note, one step at a time. Left and right copy each other until you
  type a different number for one side.
- Weight chart with a trend line that smooths out daily ups and downs.
- Customize: choose spots, one side or both, your own extra spots, body
  fat %, a curvy or straight figure, and measuring days (they appear on
  My week with a Measure button).
- Progress photos (front, side, back), stored only on this device, shown
  side by side to compare. Optional blur; included in backups (can be
  turned off). Export measurements as CSV.
- On phones, Settings moved to the gear at the top right.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.8
- Share routines and your own exercises: Share on a routine card or on
  your exercise. Send the link (or a file). Friends open it, see a
  preview and add it with one tap. The routine travels inside the link
  after the "#", which is never sent to any server. Your private notes
  are left out, and weights only if you tick "Include my weights".
- A welcome screen the very first time Forma opens on a device (never
  again after that), with units and colours.
- No outside connections: the Google web fonts are gone, Forma uses the
  device's own font.
- Fix: the bell and beeps went silent after switching to a music app.
- Developer options: tap the version number in Settings > About seven
  times and enter the code. Unlocks secret themes (Kitty) on that device.

FOR WHOEVER EDITS THE CODE NEXT
source/ is the real app and runs as-is: open source/index.html.
Build with:   python3 build.py
  docs/        the installable web app that GitHub Pages serves
  Forma.html   the single-file edition
  source/index.html        page shell and navigation
  source/style.css         all styles; colours are tokens with a dark theme
  source/js/library.js     built-in exercises, one per line (IDs never change)
  source/js/util.js        DOM, dates, units (stored in kg and metres)
  source/js/model.js       categories, equipment, templates, routine compiler
  source/js/store.js       saving, loading, validation, v1 migration, backups
  source/js/stats.js       history maths: weekly totals, streaks, records
  source/js/photos.js      progress photos in IndexedDB (not in the main save)
  source/js/ui.js          routing, dialogs, toasts, icons, body map
  source/js/views/*.js     one file per screen
  source/js/player.js      the session player (also carry-over and notes)
  source/js/views/body.js  body measurements: figure, charts, check-ins
  source/js/share.js       share links and files for routines and exercises
  source/js/stickers.js    the Kitty theme's drawings and their layout
  tools/palettes.py        the colour themes (Forest is the base tokens in
                           style.css). Edit, then: python3 tools/palettes.py
                           --write  (checks contrast, updates style.css)
  source/js/pwa.js         install, offline, updates, storage protection
  source/js/main.js        event wiring and start-up
  source/sw.js             offline cache (build.py stamps its version)
  source/manifest.webmanifest, source/icons/   app name, icon, shortcuts
Clicks are routed by data-action="…" to the actions object, forms by id to
the forms object, and inputs by data-bind to the binds object.

Exercise instructions are general guidance, not medical or personal
training advice.
